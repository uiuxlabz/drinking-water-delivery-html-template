Our story

One spring. One loop. No shortcuts.

SOURA began in 2009 with a stubborn idea: water shouldn't need a factory. So we found a spring worth protecting and built a delivery service around it.

The source

18 years underground, then straight to you

Rain falls on a granite ridge and filters down through rock for nearly two decades. By the time it surfaces at our spring it's clear, balanced and naturally soft — we don't touch the mineral profile.

We bottle only what the spring gives. In dry summers that means smaller drops, not diluted water.

The loop

We take the bottles back

Every jug is washed at 82°C, inspected and refilled up to fifty times. When it finally retires, the glass is remade into the next one. Nothing leaves the neighbourhood as waste.

Questions

Honest answers

Where exactly is the spring?
In the northern highlands, on protected land with no agriculture upstream. The coordinates are on every label and we run monthly open visits.
Why glass and not aluminium?
Glass keeps the taste true and survives dozens of wash cycles. Aluminium liners wear and can't be inspected as closely. For water, glass wins on both flavour and reuse.
What if I go on holiday?
Pause in two taps. Your jug stays with you, your plan freezes, and we skip the drop until you're back — no fees.
How often are you tested?
Every batch is sampled at the spring and again at the depot each week by an outside lab. The latest sheet is always on the Journal.

Curious enough to taste it?

Your first 10-litre jug is free. Keep it, or send it back — we're not fussy, just confident.
