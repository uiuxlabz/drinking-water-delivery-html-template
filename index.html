One watershed

We don't blend. We protect.

SOURA draws from a single granite watershed in the northern highlands — no mixing, no reprocessing. The water spends 18 years filtering through rock before it reaches you, arriving exactly as the spring made it.

  • Protected catchment, no agriculture upstream
  • Naturally mineralised — no added salts
  • Chilled, never heat-treated
